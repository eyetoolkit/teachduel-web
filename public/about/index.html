One console, three free classroom game sites.

TeachDuel is the teacher console for the Duel Family — NumeriDuel, BoardDuel, MemoryDuel. Pick a game, create a room, share the code. That's it.

Why we built it

TeachDuel started as a single math game for a single classroom. Then teachers asked for board games. Then for knowledge quizzes. Today, three sites share one console — and one promise: no student is ever identified to us.

Students enter classrooms using codes they pick themselves — S07, DOG-EARS, anything. Those codes are stored locally in each browser. Teachers see them. We do not.

What we mean by "no student identity"

  • No student names
  • No student emails or phones
  • No school IDs, government IDs, birthdays
  • No IP addresses persisted (rate-limit only)
  • No behavioral tracking, no third-party cookies, no fingerprinting
  • No AI risk-scoring on students (rejected by design)

What we won't do

  • No ads. No third-party trackers. No behavioral advertising.
  • No "premium" tier. Free forever.
  • No AI risk-scoring of students.
  • No data sharing with third parties. EU residency for EU users.

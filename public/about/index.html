§ ABOUT · Why we exist

One console, three sites, zero student identity data.

TeachDuel started as a single math game for a single classroom. Then teachers asked for board games. Then for knowledge quizzes. Today, three sites share one console — and one promise: no student is ever identified to us.

What we mean by "no student identity"

Students enter classrooms using codes they pick themselves — S07, DOG-EARS, anything. Those codes are stored locally in each browser. Teachers see them. We do not. We never see names, emails, school IDs, IP addresses, or behavioral fingerprints.

Why three sites in one console?

  • NumeriDuel — logic, math, sudoku. Five games.
  • BoardDuel — strategy, board games. Five games.
  • MemoryDuel — vocabulary, history, science. Six languages.

Teachers don't want three dashboards. They want one room-code flow that works anywhere. The console aggregates results across all three sites so a single "Class weak spot" view shows where each student is stuck — math, strategy, or recall.

What we won't do

  • No ads. No third-party trackers. No cross-site fingerprinting.
  • No "premium" tier that gates basic features. Free forever.
  • No behavioral advertising. No data sharing with third parties.
  • No AI risk-scoring of students (rejected by design — see our privacy page).

Built by

TeachDuel is a small studio. We make classroom games because we think the next generation of educational software should default to collect less, not more.

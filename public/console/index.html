Privacy we promise

We are FERPA-light & GDPR-friendly by design — the simplest privacy posture in classroom games.

What we DON'T collect

  • ❌ Student names
  • ❌ Student emails / phones
  • ❌ Student IDs (school ID, gov ID, SSN)
  • ❌ IP addresses (logged only for rate-limit, not stored beyond session)
  • ❌ Behavioral tracking / cross-site cookies

What we DO collect

  • ✓ Anonymous class codes (e.g. S07)
  • ✓ Per-question solved/duration (no PII)
  • ✓ Teacher account email (if you signed up)
  • ✓ Auto-deleted after 180 days (180-day retention)

Our full privacy policy lives at /privacy/. EU / GDPR users: we never transfer data outside EU for processing.

FERPA-light, GDPR-friendly by design.

Full list of what we collect, what we don't, and how to take your data out.

What we don't collect

  • Student names. Students identify with self-chosen codes (S07, DOG-EARS, anything).
  • Student emails, phone numbers, parent emails. Not at signup, not at any later point.
  • School IDs, government IDs, birthdays. Never requested, never stored.
  • IP addresses. Logged only for rate-limiting during the request; not persisted beyond the request.
  • Behavioral tracking. No third-party cookies, no cross-site fingerprinting, no analytics IDs.
  • AI risk-scoring. We do not run predictive models on students. Rejected by design.

GDPR · EU users

  • Lawful basis: Article 6(1)(a) consent for the teacher account; Article 6(1)(f) legitimate interest for the educational record.
  • Data residency: Stored in EU-region Cloudflare KV. Never transferred outside EU for processing.
  • Your rights: Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability. Email privacy@teachduel.com to exercise.
  • DPO contact: dpo@teachduel.com

FERPA · US schools

We do not receive student education records from schools. Teachers upload anonymous class codes voluntarily. Records are not used for any commercial purpose. We do not act as a "school official" with a legitimate educational interest.

COPPA · under 13

Our service does not target children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a teacher using TeachDuel with younger students, you do so under your school's authorization.

How to take your data out

  1. Open the Privacy tab in the teacher console (top right of any class view).
  2. Click Export my data to download a JSON archive of all classes, assignments, and student answers.
  3. Click Delete my account & data to wipe everything immediately. This cannot be undone.

If either button is greyed out, email privacy@teachduel.com and we'll process your request manually within 30 days.

Cookies

We set two cookies: a session cookie (so you don't have to log in on every page) and an anonymous teacher ID. No tracking cookies. No third-party cookies.
